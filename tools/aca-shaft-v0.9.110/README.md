# Bounded bilateral anterior A4 shaft fits, v0.9.110

The anterior A4 shafts are fitted between the short callosal roots and A3 junctions. Compact source-Y support is -35 to -27 mm, with plateau -32.5 to -30 mm. The left plateau moves 2.3 mm medially and 1 mm inferiorly; the right moves 1.5 mm medially and 1 mm inferiorly. All eight native interfaces and their 647 shared coordinates stay fixed. Earlier A4 fit regions, both A5 fits, falcine roots and all other meshes are retained. Source vertices and triangles remain exact. Plateau penetration decreases on both sides; residual embedding remains and G05 stays open.

Copy anatomy/source/aca-shaft-v0.9.110 to WORK before replay, preserving recorded evidence. Run from the app root with absolute WORK and CACHE paths.

- `reconstruct.py WORK CACHE` reads both changes in spec.json and replays the checked decoded source overrides. A quintic bump gives a unit-determinant analytical shear with Y fixed. Every original face must retain positive orientation; source weld partitions, topology and boundary positions must stay unchanged. Each changed coordinate is checked against all 766 source arterial meshes, then against the other final candidate. Flank normals use the inverse transpose of the analytical Jacobian and one SNORM16 encoding. Fixed-vertex and plateau normals stay exact. All three buffers must match accepted byte for byte.
- `validate_crossings.py WORK CACHE` checks all changed triangles against the named scene, including candidate-to-candidate, self and adjacent faces. Closed cortical contacts are deferred to the paired-depth gate. Every new non-neural crossing fails. The accepted contacts involve only the left and right middle-anterior cingulate surfaces; there are no inherited non-neural crossings.
- `validate_depth.py WORK CACHE` aggregates a complete paired check for each changed node. Matching face corners, three edge midpoints and centroids must show no new inside samples and no additional depth above 0.001 mm. Signed depth must agree with enclosed-volume parity above 0.0001 mm. Open targets require conservative empty-ball separation. If a ball centred on the first corner is inconclusive, clearance.py centres it on the triangle centroid and refines only still-inconclusive subtriangles. Distance minus enclosing radius must exceed 0.00001 mm for every leaf, and leaf area coverage must equal the original triangle area. Contact or exhaustion of the refinement limit fails; no open-target inside/outside assumption is made. The real-data centroid balls certify both superior frontal targets without subdivision. Analytic plane tests cover near, crossing and touching triangles. The predefined plateau is reported separately. Reduced sampled plateau penetration is not proof of continuous clearance; residual plateau and transition embedding remains.
- `decode-normals.mjs SOURCE_GLB WORK/decoded` extracts original SNORM16 values listed in decoded/normal-inputs.json. Historical SOURCE_GLB is the v0.9.109 complete-circulation.glb.
- `export.mjs WORK SOURCE_GLB OUTPUT_GLB` requires passing native-validation.json. It exports lossless Float32 positions, source face indices and candidate SNORM16 normal integers without octahedral re-quantisation. `verify-export.mjs WORK [OUTPUT_GLB]` needs WORK/old-complete-circulation.glb and independently checks all accepted buffers, retained physical-face corner normals, every source face index, 597 unselected meshes and four other GLBs.
- `projections.py WORK CACHE` refreshes the two selected recipes using unchanged source face lineage. Sanitised flips/removals, cap coordinates and connectivity stay exact; cap rims stay fixed. Both proxies must remain closed, oriented and positive-volume. The 826 other recipes stay unchanged.
- Preserve v0.9.109 flow as WORK/old-dsa-flow.json. Refresh only the two IDs in selected-course-ids.json, then run `verify-flow.mjs WORK`. All 595 other courses, selectability, directed connections, graph, vein sites, generator and unrelated edge distances must stay exact.
