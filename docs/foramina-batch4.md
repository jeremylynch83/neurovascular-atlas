# Skull foramina batch 4, v0.9.137

This batch fits the orbital apex and frontoethmoidal passages to the supplied teaching model. It changes three bone meshes (sphenoid, frontal and right maxilla) and eight arterial meshes. It adds a cranial optic-canal aperture marker on each side. Existing optic, fissure, ethmoidal and optic-strut markers are refitted.

## Implemented

- Bilateral common optic canals with thin reference-guided bony roof reconstruction over the native canal extent, an inferolateral ophthalmic arterial recess and independently fitted cranial and orbital apertures. The main ophthalmic arterial surfaces are retained. The optic nerve is not segmented.
- Native elongated superior orbital fissures, with local rim clearance for the medial arterial routes and existing ophthalmic venous surface. The optic and maxillary struts are retained and tested with independent bone controls.
- Native inferior orbital fissures, posterior entry guides and local relief of the right infraorbital venous rim contact. The full anterior infraorbital groove and canal remain for batch 6.
- Separate anterior and posterior ethmoidal routes through the orbital roof, with the arterial course fitted to the corresponding canal or groove. The ethmoid mesh itself is unchanged. Local coverage in this model does not establish a population rule about ethmoidal canal dehiscence.
- Anterior and posterior ethmoidal arteries, deep recurrent ophthalmic arteries and arteries of the superior orbital fissure fitted bilaterally. The latter had pre-existing folded surfaces and are rebuilt from their exact native maxillary mouths, with the native projected cross-section retained, an illustrative 0.18 mm median shaft radius and 0.12 mm distal taper.
- A rounded terminal DSA guide point in the unchanged right superficial recurrent meningeal branch moved 0.02 mm inside its existing cap. Its actual geometry is unchanged.
- Twelve surface-to-surface endpoint checks retain the existing orbital potential links. The left fissure artery tip extends locally to meet its existing anteromedial ILT link.

## Verification and limits

The accompanying JSON reports record regional vessel-bone contacts, non-adjacent arterial surface contacts, retained strut controls, exact unchanged mesh arrays, native parent interfaces and closed DSA injection sections. The package also includes the production build and prior-batch DSA regression results. These are model consistency checks, not clinical validation or patient-specific CT segmentation.

The broad cribriform region is retained. Fine olfactory perforations, optional accessory optic and meningolacrimal variants remain for batch 7. Full anterior infraorbital canal work remains for batch 6. Four planned batches remain; see [the batch plan](skull-foramina-batch-plan.md).
