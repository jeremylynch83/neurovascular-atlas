# Revised vertebral segment starts and ASA origins, v0.8.12

Jeremy Lynch’s image(20261003-233148).png supplies the revised black V3/V4 start lines and the red ASA-origin mark. The marked right-sided stations are transferred to the corresponding left-sided course.

V1 and its existing V2 boundary are retained. V3 now includes the marked upper cervical limb and atlas loop; V4 starts at the marked ascending cranial limb and continues to the basilar junction. This supersedes the v0.8.9 boundary placement.

Stations are measured along the retained reference centrelines. Screenshot localisation is approximate; the hand marks define the intended levels and do not establish a measured dural plane.

The paired ASA ostia move proximally along the retained V4 courses. Each ASA root and its shared vertebral seam move together. The central ASA confluence, its two seam rings and the descending ASA remain fixed. All other branch collars stay fixed. A constrained triangulation of each affected vertebral wall patch preserves its boundary edges, centreline, calibre and triangle count. Duplicate copies of an earlier segment seam are welded for that local topology.

Posterior spinal and posterior meningeal VA branches are grouped under V3 to reflect their retained physical origins and the revised boundary. Structure IDs, names, description text and links are retained. The description catalogue’s parent links are updated to match.

No new GUI or runtime validation is added. One-off authoring records are in `validation/va-asa-v0.8.12.json`; surface and viewer checks accompany the release. Bones, ICA/ACA sections, labial/submental corrections, potential connections and GUI source remain unchanged.
