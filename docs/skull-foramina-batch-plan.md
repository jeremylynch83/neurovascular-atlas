|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sphenoid and middle cranial fossa | Implemented, v0.9.134 |

Each implementation is a reference-guided reconstruction of this teaching model. Remaining batches are not represented as completed or clinically validated.

All eight planned batches are complete. The final broad screen identified residual vascular/bone contacts and inherited source-mesh defects beyond the earlier passage windows. They are prioritised in `skull-foramina-residual-issues.md`; completion of this programme is not unqualified whole-model anatomical acceptance. Source-limited microcanals and unselected variants retain their documented status.
