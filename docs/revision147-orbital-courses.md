# Revision 0.9.147: lower optic canals and orbital arterial routes

The previous upper optic openings are closed by restoring the source lesser-wing surface. Each new oval canal is fitted to the paired lower source indentation and traced to the orbital apex. This is a reference-guided teaching reconstruction, not patient CT validation. Optic nerve geometry is not supplied.

The nominal bore is 4.5 x 3.7 mm, with approximately 5.1-5.2 mm fully enclosed. Only the sphenoid is modified among the craniofacial display nodes. A 0.005 mm simplification tolerance removes Boolean slivers; inherited face shading is retained.

The ophthalmic arteries pass through the new canals. The short proximal attachment and its shared arterial interfaces are shifted posteriorly by 3 mm. The distal orbital tree is retained. The left superior orbital fissure artery is moved 0.6 mm medially and 0.3 mm upwards at its orbital turn. The right SOF artery already traversed the fissure and is retained. Deep recurrent ophthalmic branches are fitted through the medial fissure with their parent and terminal ports held.

All measured coincident branch interfaces remain coincident. Targeted ophthalmic, SOF and deep recurrent ophthalmic meshes have no sphenoid intersections in the delivered model. The right ophthalmic mesh retains its pre-existing tiny distal self contacts; no new material self crossings were introduced in the fitted courses. Other pre-existing atlas contacts and incomplete anatomical target flags are not declared resolved by this revision.

31 vascular nodes are changed without changing triangle winding or connectivity; 1167 other vascular/brain nodes are exactly retained. Flow guides and closed projection surfaces are updated with their meshes and tested separately. All 28 updated courses passed, including 96 closed split sections.

Reference: Kiyosue et al. Artery of the Superior Orbital Fissure, 2015. PMID 26206808. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26206808/
