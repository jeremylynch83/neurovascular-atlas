# v0.2 vascular seed

v0.2 is the first release with visible vascular geometry.

## Geometry included

The following structures are imported from the Z-Anatomy neurovascular curves already present in the Dental Scope source pipeline, registered to the legacy BodyParts3D jaw/skull frame:

- bilateral external carotid arteries
- bilateral facial arteries
- bilateral maxillary arteries
- bilateral inferior alveolar arteries
- bilateral posterior superior alveolar arteries
- bilateral descending palatine arteries
- bilateral buccal arteries
- bilateral internal jugular veins
- bilateral retromandibular veins
- bilateral maxillary veins
- bilateral facial veins

These assets are **atlas-derived seed geometry**. They are not CTA segmentations and must not be treated as the final INR master anatomy. The original Dental Scope pipeline sampled Z-Anatomy Bezier curves and registered the atlas jaws to BodyParts3D with a similarity ICP. v0.2 preserves that provenance rather than relabelling the geometry as scan-derived.

## Deliberately not imported

The old Dental Scope schematic pterygoid plexus and inferior alveolar vein are not imported. The INR atlas should not gain apparently precise geometry from structures that were explicitly schematic in the starter project.

## Next data gate

The next geometry milestone remains image-derived anatomy: a suitable CTA/MRA/CTV reference dataset, source-volume QA, master coordinate frame, skull extraction and intracranial arterial/venous geometry. The logical catalogue already contains those structures, but they remain `planned` until that gate is passed.
